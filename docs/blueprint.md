# **App Name**: ChronoSync

## Core Features:

- AI-Powered Scheduling: Suggest optimal times for events/tasks based on user data and priorities. This will incorporate a tool to intelligently consider whether a certain scheduled time should incorporate extra time to complete certain tasks
- Conflict Detection & Resolution: Proactively identify scheduling conflicts and offer intelligent solutions using predictive analytics.
- Task Prioritization Engine: Prioritize tasks, influencing scheduling and time allocation decisions, to enable a recommendation of whether or not to allocate extra time for task completion
- Calendar Synchronization: Seamlessly sync with Google, Outlook, and Apple Calendars.
- Team Collaboration Features: Enable shared calendars, availability views, and collaborative scheduling for group tasks and meetings.
- Productivity Analytics Dashboard: Visualizations and reports on time allocation, productivity patterns, and goal achievement.
- User Profile & Preferences: Allow users to customize work hours, break times, preferred meeting durations, and AI sensitivity.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#2962FF) for a sense of calm, focus, and productivity.
- Background color: Light gray (#F5F7FA) to provide a clean and unobtrusive backdrop.
- Accent color: Bright purple (#A259FF) to highlight important actions and AI-driven suggestions.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' for a modern and neutral feel suitable for both headlines and body text.
- Clean and simple line icons for easy recognition of events, tasks, and settings.
- Dashboard layout with clear sections for calendar overview, task list, and AI insights.
- Subtle transitions and animations to guide users through interactions without being distracting.
